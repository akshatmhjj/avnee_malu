## Overview

AI Portfolio Doctor is a portfolio analytics project built to make investment risk easier to read. It combines return analysis, risk measures, and technical indicators in one workflow so that portfolio behaviour can be reviewed before making an allocation decision.

## Analysis included

- Sharpe ratio to compare risk-adjusted performance.
- Annualised volatility to show how widely returns move over time.
- Maximum drawdown to highlight the depth of past portfolio declines.
- Simple Moving Average and Relative Strength Index analysis to add a market-momentum view.
- Portfolio stress testing to examine risk exposure and performance during adverse market conditions.

## Decision value

A portfolio can look strong on returns alone while carrying more volatility or downside risk than expected. This project brings the measures together in a simple, decision-oriented view, helping an investor ask better questions about diversification, risk tolerance, and allocation.

## Project impact

The workflow turns separate performance and risk measures into one repeatable review process. It helps compare portfolios on both upside participation and downside behaviour, making risk conversations more concrete before capital is allocated.
