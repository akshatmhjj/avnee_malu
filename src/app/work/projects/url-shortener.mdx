## Overview

This project brings together the type of analysis used during my financial analyst internship at Radiant Metal. The goal was to understand where margin was being created or lost, then turn that view into recommendations for pricing, budgeting, and procurement.

## Analysis completed

- Evaluated pricing models and forecasted profit margins across more than five product lines.
- Compared budget expectations with actual performance to improve forecasting accuracy.
- Reviewed vendor and procurement costs to identify areas where spending could be reduced.
- Analysed month-end reporting workflows and the information needed for faster, more reliable closes.
- Presented findings in a clear format so that financial insights could support operational decisions.

## Outcome

The analysis contributed to a 12% improvement in margin accuracy, a 15% improvement in forecasting accuracy, and identified opportunities to reduce procurement costs by 10%. It also supported recommendations associated with an 8% increase in revenue.
