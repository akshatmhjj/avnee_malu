## Overview

Corporate Credit Risk Model is an interactive credit-risk analytics application built with Python and Streamlit. It uses published financial-ratio data to estimate a firm’s Probability of Default (PD), assign an illustrative risk category, calculate a transparent Financial Health Score, identify important model associations, and evaluate stress scenarios.

The application uses the UCI Taiwanese Bankruptcy Prediction dataset, containing 6,819 firm observations and 95 published financial-ratio variables. The modelling workflow selects 12 financially meaningful variables across profitability, liquidity, leverage, coverage, and growth.

> This project is designed for academic and portfolio purposes. It is not investment advice, a lending decision, or an official credit rating.

## Analysis Included

### Probability of Default and Risk Classification

- Logistic Regression model producing a predicted Probability of Default
- Illustrative risk categories: Low, Moderate, High, and Very High Risk
- Historical-observation selection for individual firm-level analysis
- Project-specific thresholds clearly distinguished from regulatory or banking standards

### Financial Health Analysis

The application evaluates financially relevant published ratios across:

- Profitability: ROA, operating profit rate, and net income to assets
- Liquidity: current ratio, quick ratio, and working capital to assets
- Leverage: debt ratio and total debt to net worth
- Coverage and cash generation: cash flow to assets and interest coverage
- Growth and scale: asset growth and revenue per share

### Model Evaluation

The project compares an interpretable Logistic Regression model with a Random Forest classifier using a stratified held-out test set.

- Logistic Regression: ROC-AUC of 0.935 and PR-AUC of 0.421
- Random Forest: ROC-AUC of 0.938 and F1-score of 0.359
- Evaluation includes precision, recall, F1-score, confusion matrix, ROC curve, and precision-recall curve
- Class imbalance is addressed with class-weighted model training

### Risk Driver Analysis

For each selected observation, the dashboard ranks standardized Logistic Regression contributions to show which variables are associated with higher or lower predicted default risk.

These outputs are presented as model associations rather than causal claims.

### Stress Testing

The application applies illustrative ratio-level stress scenarios:

- Base Case: no change in revenue, profitability, or debt
- Mild Stress: lower revenue and profitability with higher leverage
- Severe Stress: larger declines in revenue and profitability with increased leverage

Each scenario recalculates affected financial inputs, generates a stressed PD, measures the PD change, and compares the baseline and stressed risk categories.

## Financial Health Score

Corporate Credit Risk Model produces a transparent 0–100 Financial Health Score based on historical percentiles of profitability, liquidity, leverage, and cash-flow indicators.

Higher profitability, liquidity, and cash generation improve the score, while higher leverage reduces it. The Financial Health Score is a companion diagnostic and does not replace the model-generated Probability of Default.

## Decision Value

A firm may appear profitable while carrying high leverage, weak liquidity, limited cash-flow capacity, or sensitivity to adverse operating conditions. This project combines default-risk modelling, financial-ratio diagnostics, explainability, and stress testing in one repeatable workflow to support structured corporate credit-risk analysis.
