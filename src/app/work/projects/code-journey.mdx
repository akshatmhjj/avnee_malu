## Overview

This project builds a full three-statement financial model for Chipotle using FY2022–FY2024 Form 10-K data and FY2025E–FY2029E forecasts. The model translates restaurant-level drivers into a connected view of revenue, profitability, cash flow, balance sheet strength, and share-count changes.

## What the model covers

- Forecasts restaurant openings, comparable sales, revenue, margins, and operating expenses.
- Links the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement so that changes in operating assumptions flow through the full model.
- Includes supporting schedules for leases, property and equipment, equity, and share count.
- Uses Base, Bull, and Bear cases to show how different operating outcomes affect the business.

## Quality checks

The model includes 40 internal checks and 129 independent audit checks. These checks test statement tie-outs, forecast consistency, balance sheet balance, and the integrity of key calculations.

## Decision value

The project demonstrates how a well-structured model can move beyond a collection of spreadsheet outputs. By linking business drivers to financial statements and scenarios, it creates a practical tool for evaluating performance, assumptions, and downside risk.

## Project impact

The model gives an investor a clear line of sight from restaurant openings and comparable sales to earnings, cash generation, leverage, and share count. Its audit checks also make the forecast easier to review, challenge, and update.
